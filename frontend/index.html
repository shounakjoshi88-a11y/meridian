Triage

Rank candidate conditions from reported symptoms. Matching is set algebra over the knowledge base, not a diagnosis.

Separate with commas or pipes.

Optional. Used to check contraindications.

No symptoms entered

List the symptoms a patient reports and the knowledge base will be ranked by how much each condition explains them.

Patients

Records held in data/patients.csv. Search tolerates incomplete entries and returns a ranked list.

Matches name, contact, address and notes. Open a record for its consultation history.

Hospitals

Facilities in data/hospitals.csv, with the doctors the registry lists as practising there.

Matches name, type, area, city and accreditation.

Doctors

Practitioners in data/doctors.csv, each attached to the hospital they work at.

Matches name, specialisation, qualification, department and hospital.

Medicines

The catalogue in data/medicines.csv. Each medicine lists which medical stores hold it.

Matches name, generic name, category and manufacturer.

Stores

Medical stores and what they hold. A store with no recorded stock shows as empty rather than being treated as stocking nothing.

Matches name, type, area, city and opening hours.

Cohorts

Public clinical datasets profiled with pandas. Only datasets that met every selection criterion are served.

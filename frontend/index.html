Patients

Every registered patient. Open a record for its consultation history, or search to narrow the list.

Matches name, contact, address and notes. Leave this empty to keep browsing the list.

Hospitals

Empanelled facilities, with the doctors the registry lists as practising there.

Matches name, type, area, city and accreditation. Leave this empty to keep browsing the list.

Doctors

Practitioners, each attached to the hospital they work at. Open one for their registration and schedule.

Matches name, specialisation, qualification, department and hospital. Leave this empty to keep browsing the list.

Medicines

The catalogue. Each medicine lists which medical stores hold it.

Matches name, generic name, category and manufacturer. Leave this empty to keep browsing the list.

Stores

Medical stores and what they hold. A store with nothing recorded says so rather than reading as empty shelves.

Matches name, type, area, city and opening hours. Leave this empty to keep browsing the list.

Rare conditions

Every disease the Human Phenotype Ontology annotates, with the phenotypic findings recorded for it. Built from hp.obo and phenotype.hpoa, filtered to findings a clinician would recognise.

Searches 11,000+ diseases and 11,000+ distinct findings. One word can match thousands, so results are capped and the true total is shown.
